What countries do you provide visa consultancy for?+

Services cover a range of destinations, including the United Kingdom, Schengen Area countries, the UAE, Malaysia, Turkey, Saudi Arabia and Qatar. Contact us to discuss a specific destination.

How long does a visa application typically take?+

Timelines vary by destination and visa category. The source site gives typical estimates of 5–15 working days for tourist visas, 3–8 weeks for student visas and 4–12 weeks for work permits. A case-specific timeline is discussed at consultation.

What is your visa approval rate?+

The source site reports an overall approval rate of around 95%. Outcomes depend on individual eligibility, documentation and the relevant authority; no result can be guaranteed.
